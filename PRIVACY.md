# Privacy Policy - EvarTheme Shifter

Last updated: 19 November 2026

EvarTheme Shifter is a browser extension that adds dark mode to your college portal and LMS. This policy explains what it does with your data.

## What we collect
Nothing. EvarTheme Shifter does not collect, store, send, sell, or share any personal data, browsing history, passwords, marks, or any other information.

## What it does on a page
When you open the college portal or LMS, the extension changes only the appearance of that page in your own browser: background, text, link, input, table and card colours. It does not read your login details, your courses, your marks, or any other content, and it does not send anything anywhere.

## Settings
Your on/off state, theme and brightness are saved only in Chrome sync storage on your own Google account, so they follow your Chrome profile. Nothing is sent to us.

## Third parties
No third-party services, no analytics, no tracking, no ads.

## Contact
If you have a question about this policy, contact the developer through the store listing.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published with the next extension update.
